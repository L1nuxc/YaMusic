# Music app design concepts

Generated with the built-in image_gen tool. Five static visual directions, not an implemented app. Each image shows desktop/web on the left and mobile on the right. Dark theme primary; light theme planned.

## Shared prompt

Use case: ui-mockup.
Create ONE polished high fidelity landscape design concept board for a personal music streaming service. Show EXACTLY TWO coordinated interfaces side by side on the SAME image: a complete desktop/web application on the LEFT and a portrait mobile application on the RIGHT, as a clear split-screen comparison. Large readable UI, crisp straight-on screens, no perspective distortion, no people or desks. Desktop and mobile must unmistakably share the same design system. Both show the home screen with music recommendation content. Main dark theme. Palette strictly near-black, charcoal, neutral grays and white; only album artwork may have very restrained color. Beautiful consistent bespoke rounded outline icons, same stroke weight everywhere, polished typography, generous spacing, excellent contrast. iOS Liquid Glass inspiration translated into an original understated cross-platform product, no Apple logo. Glass is functional and restrained; labels are legible. Russian UI exact short labels: "Главная", "Поиск", "Медиатека", "Моя волна", "Микс дня", "Нейро-волна". Include a text-prompt entry for the neural wave, a few tasteful original album covers, a persistent player with artwork, play/pause, next, heart, progress. Desktop has sidebar navigation and bottom player; mobile has bottom navigation and compact player above it. Show a small moon/sun theme switch in profile/settings access to imply dark and light themes, keep all displayed screens DARK. No feature overload or tiny dense text. No code, no implementation annotations, no watermarks. Include only the specified variant number/name at the top of the board. Finished presentation suitable for choosing a visual direction; physically plausible glass and restrained dimensionality.

## 01 — Graphite Glass

Text heading: "01 — Graphite Glass". Direction: the quietest, most precise version. Warm-neutral graphite canvas, subtle smoky transparent panels, fine light edges and 20px corner radii, understated silver accents, luxurious negative space. A large wide horizontal "Моя волна" hero card with an abstract silver liquid ripple in its right side, two smaller daily/neural cards below. Flat calm content with glass limited to sidebar, floating player, mobile bottom navigation. Desktop navigation expanded with labels. Mobile hero vertically reflows, album rows below. Elegant restrained soft depth, no glow. This should feel like an exceptionally refined minimal everyday music app.

## 02 — Frosted Cloud

Text heading: "02 — Frosted Cloud". Direction: soft and buoyant, heavily frosted opaque charcoal glass, friendly large rounded squircle cards, pill-shaped controls, smooth blurred neutral-gray cloud forms behind the wave hero. Very soft shadows and diffuse internal highlights, no sharp chrome or dramatic reflections. A modular bento home: big "Моя волна" card, smaller "Микс дня" and "Нейро-волна" tiles, rounded music thumbnails. Compact sidebar icons in soft rounded squares, floating broad capsule player. Mobile uses the same soft bento geometry and an especially soft floating pill navigation. Keep dark theme and bright readable white labels. Suggest gently bouncy interaction through shape design, without motion arrows.

## 03 — Crystal Dock

Text heading: "03 — Crystal Dock". Direction: the clearest expression of restrained liquid glass. Deep almost-black canvas, clean highly translucent crystal sidebar and floating bottom dock with visible edge refraction, slender bright rim highlights, very subtle liquid lenses on selected controls. Central recommendation content mostly unboxed to keep it minimal. "Моя волна" is a calm large circular translucent liquid-glass play lens surrounded by white type and abstract gray waveform. "Микс дня" album rail underneath, "Нейро-волна" sleek glass prompt field. Very thin consistent outline icons. Mobile repeats the circular wave control and a carefully rendered floating liquid glass bottom dock, with compact player attached above. No rainbow gradients, excessive sparkle, or unreadable transparency.

## 04 — Mono Editorial

Text heading: "04 — Mono Editorial". Direction: graphic monochrome editorial minimalism with confident oversized typography, almost flat black/gray surfaces and precise grid, small 8–12px rounded corners. Very restrained glass only in player and selected navigation pill. Large typographic "Моя волна" feature dominates the home, abstract monochrome soundwave in a clean rectangular area; horizontal album cards, clean track lists, understated silver hairline separators. Asymmetric spacious desktop composition, very strong hierarchy. Mobile adapts the typography and precise alignment, rectangular album art, simple tabs, tidy player. White primary play button, elegant compact thin icons, no huge 3D blobs. Make this noticeably different in geometry from the soft pill and crystal styles while preserving cross-platform unity.

## 05 — Immersive Smoke

Text heading: "05 — Immersive Smoke". Direction: atmospheric and immersive yet minimal, monochrome blurred album-inspired background flowing smoothly from deep black into dark silver-gray, large airy transparent layers, restrained soft glass. Home organized around an elegant tall central "Моя волна" listening panel with abstract grayscale album art, integrated circular play control, below a horizontal "Микс дня" rail and "Нейро-волна" prompt capsule. Desktop has narrow labeled translucent navigation and a right-hand slim queue panel showing a few track rows. Mobile reflows to a tall artwork-led hero with the same backdrop and a floating glass player/nav. Emphasize quiet depth, diffuse smoke, and beautiful edge lighting, not chrome or bento. Neutral typography and icon consistency. The background must remain dark enough for high text contrast.
