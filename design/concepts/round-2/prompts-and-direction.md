## User-approved direction

- Home based on 05 Immersive Smoke, including desktop queue.
- Desktop bottom player from 01 Graphite Glass, with corrected alignment and centered volume controls.
- Desktop sidebar and mobile capsule navigation from 02 Frosted Cloud.
- Calm monochrome palette from 04 Mono Editorial, with softer frosted materials from 02.

## Intended motion, to be explored later

Mobile capsule active glass lens stretches gently while held and dragged, follows the finger, then settles softly on the selected tab when released. Wave background moves slowly. These PNGs do not demonstrate animation; no interactive prototype has been implemented.

## Shared generation prompt

Use case: ui-mockup. Create a refined SECOND-ROUND high-fidelity music app concept, combining ONLY the user-approved parts of the four references. One wide landscape board, TWO interfaces side by side: desktop/web on left, portrait mobile on right. Straight-on, crisp readable UI, no desks/hands/perspective. This is an evolution of reference 1, not a new unrelated style.

REFERENCE ROLES:
Image 1 (05 Immersive Smoke): PRIMARY home composition reference. Preserve the atmospheric main wave hero, central artwork above "Моя волна", restrained playback controls, desktop right queue "Далее в волне", daily mix rail, neural-wave input lower down. Make the main play button modestly sized, never enormous.
Image 2 (01 Graphite Glass): use ONLY its DESKTOP BOTTOM PLAYER. Preserve the multi-zone arrangement: left album thumbnail + two text lines + heart, CENTER transport controls with a separate long progress bar BELOW them, right elapsed/total time + speaker + short volume slider. Fix alignment, equal control spacing and consistent baselines. In particular speaker and volume slider vertically centered on the transport-button row. Do NOT use its home cards or mobile player.
Image 3 (02 Frosted Cloud): use ONLY its desktop LEFT SIDEBAR and MOBILE BOTTOM NAVIGATION, plus soft frosted material inspiration. Desktop sidebar has small waveform mark, labels "Главная", "Поиск", "Медиатека", softly rounded selected item and profile/settings access at bottom. Mobile bottom nav is a SINGLE floating dark frosted glass capsule with three evenly spaced icons and tiny labels, and a translucent rounded active lens INSIDE the capsule, visibly refracting the backdrop. It is intended to gently stretch under finger drag like liquid glass; depict a plausible polished resting state, no fake motion arrows.
Image 4 (04 Mono Editorial): use ONLY its calm neutral black/charcoal/gray/white palette and contrast. Do not adopt its oversized typography or home layout.

CRITICAL USER CHANGES:
Absolutely NO theme toggles anywhere on either home screen: no sun/moon icons, no light/dark switch. Theme control lives only inside settings, which are not shown. A subtle gear/profile access is okay.
Phone must be uncluttered and have VERY LITTLE text: main "Моя волна", no explanatory paragraphs, daily mix covers with very short titles, "Нейро-волна" with just "Опишите настроение..." in its field. Keep the desktop "Далее в волне" queue but do not squeeze it onto the phone.
Phone MINI PLAYER must be compact and tidy: small square artwork, one short track title and small artist line, heart, pause, next; a hairline progress indicator along the bottom edge, no large draggable slider, no timer clutter. Mini player sits ABOVE and separate from bottom navigation with a small gap, no overlaps, safe bottom inset.
Maintain unified elegant rounded outline icons, identical stroke width, white/soft-gray text, high contrast and correct Russian labels. Album art may have quiet restrained colors but UI stays monochrome. Glass selective, not a bright border around everything. Beautiful spacing and optical alignment. No marketing paragraphs, no watermarks. Do not show other pages or extra devices. Only a short variant heading above the board. Static visual mockup, not code.
